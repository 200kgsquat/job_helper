[Model Comparison Report]

Dataset: Industry Classification (10 classes)
Test Samples: 1,591

Overall Metrics:
+----------------+-----------+-----------+
| Metric         | BERT      | TF-IDF+LR |
+----------------+-----------+-----------+
| Accuracy       | 74.86%    | 73.98%    |
| Macro F1-Score | 77.19%    | 76.43%    |
+----------------+-----------+-----------+

Key Observations:

1. Performance Difference:
- BERT slightly outperforms TF-IDF+LR in both accuracy (+0.88%) and macro F1-score (+0.76%)
- Both models show similar overall performance patterns

2. Class-wise Analysis:
- Education Management: Both models achieve excellent performance (BERT: 0.97 F1, TF-IDF: 0.96 F1)
- Hospital & Health Care: Comparable high performance (0.89 F1 both)
- Oil & Energy: TF-IDF shows perfect precision (1.00) while BERT achieves better recall (0.91 vs 0.87)
- Telecommunications: BERT shows significantly better recall (0.69 vs 0.48) but lower precision
- Computer Software: TF-IDF achieves better balance between precision and recall
- Marketing and Advertising: TF-IDF shows more balanced performance (0.74/0.74/0.74)

3. Strengths:
BERT:
- Better handling of semantic nuances in text
- Superior performance on classes requiring contextual understanding
- More consistent recall across most categories

TF-IDF+LR:
- Computationally more efficient
- Better precision in several categories (Computer Software, Telecommunications)
- Strong performance on clearly defined industry terms

4. Weaknesses:
BERT:
- Lower precision for Marketing and Advertising (0.62)
- Higher computational requirements

TF-IDF+LR:
- Struggles with semantic variations (Telecommunications recall: 0.48)
- Less effective with complex linguistic patterns

Conclusion:
Both models achieve comparable overall performance, with BERT showing a slight advantage in overall metrics.
BERT demonstrates better contextual understanding while TF-IDF+LR remains a strong baseline for classification tasks
where computational efficiency is important. The choice between models should consider:
- Required inference speed
- Computational resources available
- Importance of precision vs recall for specific applications
